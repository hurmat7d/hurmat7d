### 🚀 What I'm working toward

My path into this started in Salesforce itself, then moved through market research and data validation — understanding how real customer data behaves outside a clean CRM — and later into financial services, where I saw firsthand how one wrong data move can carry real consequences, not just a broken report.

That combination shapes what I want to build: Salesforce that isn't just powerful for large, well-resourced teams, but genuinely usable as a first point of contact for people who aren't naturally technical — the small business owner, the ops person who's never touched a data model, the person who just needs something that works without a consultant standing next to them.

That's the gap I keep coming back to: Salesforce has genuinely made "clicks, not code" possible — but there's still real distance between that promise and something a non-technical person could actually pick up and trust on their own. Closing that distance is what I want to spend my career on.
